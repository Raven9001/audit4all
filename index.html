What it checks

Three checks, ranked High, Medium and Low. Normal things stay out of your way.

🚪

Open ports

Which programs are listening, and whether they can be reached from your network or only from your own PC.

⚙️

Startup programs

What launches by itself at boot: unsigned entries, programs running from Temp or Downloads, hidden launch tricks.

📡

Live traffic

A short capture of what your PC sends and receives: repeating check-ins, port scans, unencrypted logins.

Quick start

Under a minute from download to first result.

Download the zip from Releases and extract it.
Double-click run_audit4all.bat.
Say Yes to the administrator prompt for the full check.
Pick Desktop or Browser, then press Run audit.

Needs Python 3 with "Add python.exe to PATH" ticked. For live traffic, install Npcap from npcap.com.

Privacy

Everything runs on your PC. AI summaries are optional.

Local

Port, startup and packet checks never leave your computer.

Text only

With AI on, only a short description of findings is sent. Never packets or files.

No key, no upload

Without an AI key, nothing is sent anywhere.

Honest limits: AUDIT4ALL is a first look, not antivirus. A clean result means nothing obviously wrong was found.
